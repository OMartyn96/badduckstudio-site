Goal

Spell the word above each outlaw to take them down before they get a shot off.

Loading demo... The first load can take a little while because the WebGL build is large.

How to play

  • Use the letter wheel to build the enemy word.
  • Choose letters in the correct order to fire.
  • Act fast: wrong or slow spelling gives enemies time to attack.
